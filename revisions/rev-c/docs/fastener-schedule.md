Do not order the unresolved rows as a complete kit. M02 is open until every
selected length has a catalog order code, quantity, positive seating,
adequate engagement and tool access. Select ordinary catalog washers or
lengths when adjusting a stack; grinding screws, drilling clearance holes,
slotting plates and modifying purchased bearings are prohibited.

For a T-slot joint, measure screw projection below the mating face and
compare it with both the nut's thread engagement and the slot floor. A
screw can feel tight because it has bottomed while the joint remains loose.
For a blind tapped joint, leave positive bottom clearance and verify that
unthreaded lead-in is excluded from usable engagement. Record these checks
in the assembly traveler before threadlocker or final tightening.

Original vendor CAD is available for the Thorlabs interfaces. The Del-Tron
body is a drawing reconstruction. Its #6-32 threads are not M4. SD1 is sold
for #8/M4; the proposed #6 application has nominal bearing area, but has not
been accepted as a vendor-matched connection. Do not conceal that distinction
with a generic hardware symbol in a drawing.
